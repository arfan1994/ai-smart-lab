Understanding Earth.
Informing decisions.

SMART is a collaborative research group led by Dr. Arfan Arshad at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan.

We address water, weather, climate, and environmental challenges using remote sensing, geospatial technologies, hydrological modeling, and artificial intelligence. Our research connects scientific innovation with practical solutions for communities and ecosystems, particularly in data-scarce and climate-vulnerable regions.

Our mission is to develop reliable knowledge and technologies that support water security, environmental sustainability, and resilience to natural hazards.

Research directions

Connected questions. Complementary methods.

Students develop focused research projects across these interconnected areas, guided by scientific questions, data availability, and practical relevance.

01 / OBSERVE

Earth Observation & Remote Sensing

Use satellite observations to investigate changes in water storage, precipitation, evapotranspiration, soil moisture, snow, vegetation, and land cover.

  • GRACE/GRACE-FO
  • Satellite datasets
  • Environmental change
02 / MODEL

Hydrology & Earth Systems

Study water balances, rainfall–runoff processes, recharge, storage change, and interactions between land, atmosphere, climate, and human activities.

  • Water balance
  • Land-surface models
  • Recharge
03 / LEARN

Geospatial Artificial Intelligence

Develop and evaluate machine-learning approaches for downscaling, prediction, forecasting, spatial analysis, and transparent uncertainty assessment.

  • Explainable AI
  • Downscaling
  • Uncertainty
04 / SUSTAIN

Groundwater & Agricultural Water

Examine groundwater depletion and recharge, irrigation demand, monitoring priorities, and opportunities for sustainable water management.

  • Groundwater
  • Irrigation
  • Water security
05 / ANTICIPATE

Hydroclimatic Extremes & Risk

Investigate floods, droughts, extreme rainfall, heat, and wildfire–hydrology interactions to understand hazards and their consequences.

  • Floods & droughts
  • Extreme events
  • Risk assessment
06 / ADAPT

Climate & Environmental Sustainability

Assess how climate and land-use changes affect water, ecosystems, agriculture, and communities, and explore adaptation strategies.

  • Climate impacts
  • Ecosystems
  • Adaptation

For MS students

Learn the methods. Own the question.

We value curiosity, scientific integrity, thoughtful collaboration, and a willingness to learn.

Developing your research

A strong project starts with a clear question and grows through reading, experimentation, discussion, and revision. Topics and milestones are agreed with the advisor.

  • Connect a meaningful environmental question with appropriate data and methods.
  • Build skills in GIS, remote sensing, programming, modeling, and scientific communication.
  • Evaluate assumptions, validation quality, uncertainty, and practical limitations.
  • Produce organized analyses, well-supported writing, and reproducible research outputs.

Working together

Meetings and preparation

Agree on a meeting schedule with your advisor. Bring a concise update covering progress, evidence, challenges, decisions needed, and next steps. Record agreed actions after each meeting.

Research integrity and responsible AI

Report results honestly, cite sources, document methods, and protect confidential information. Verify AI-assisted work and disclose its use where required. Students remain responsible for the accuracy and originality of their work.

Collaboration and co-authorship

Obtain the advisor’s prior approval before initiating internal or external research collaborations, sharing research materials, or involving additional researchers. Proposed co-authors outside the team must have substantial, documented contributions that justify authorship. Discuss and approve proposed arrangements before making commitments, consistent with applicable authorship criteria.

Data, code, and communication

Preserve raw data, document processing steps, maintain backups, and use version control where practical. Share progress early, give respectful feedback, and inform the advisor promptly about delays or difficulties.
